# Oral Exam Preferences and LLM-Use Declaration

## Use of feedback

I did not use feedback provided for the laboratory assignments when completing or revising the labs.

## Use of LLM-based tools

I used LLM-based tools to assist with code generation, debugging, and understanding some of the concepts covered in the assignments. I used this support because my academic and professional background is primarily in life sciences and causal inference, rather than software engineering or natural language processing. The LLMs therefore served as coding and learning aids during the assignments.
